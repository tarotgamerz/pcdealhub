About PCDealHub

A practical PC-hardware deals and buying-guide site for India.

PCDealHub is being built to make PC shopping simpler: find relevant hardware, understand whether the price is reasonable, and get to the retailer without spending hours comparing listings.

What we cover

Graphics cardsGPU deals, used-market checks and buying guidance.
ProcessorsCPU comparisons, bottleneck guidance and platform planning.
Memory & storageRAM and SSD capacity, compatibility and value guidance.
Complete buildsBalanced component choices for different budgets and goals.

How a deal gets published

  1. We find a candidate listing.
  2. We check the seller, current price, availability, condition and relevant terms.
  3. We compare the price against a reasonable reference point.
  4. Only after those checks does a listing become eligible for the public verified-deals feed.
Transparency: PCDealHub may use affiliate links. A qualifying purchase can earn us a commission at no extra cost to the customer. Affiliate status does not replace our deal-verification checks.

What we do not do

We do not intentionally publish invented prices, fake discounts, or listings we have not verified. Prices and stock can still change after publication, so the retailer page remains the final source for checkout details.
