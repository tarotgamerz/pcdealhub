Paper trade controls

Only fresh confirmed BUY / LONG or SELL / SHORT setups qualify. Risk is capped at 0.1–1% of balance by stop distance, 5% notional per position and 20% total open notional. A verified exchange quote and bid-ask spread ≤20 bps are mandatory. Paper only. No real exchange orders are sent.
Target-only observation test: the paper position stays open after a stop touch and auto-closes only when its profit target is confirmed. If the same closed candle touches both stop and target, it stays open because the order of touch is unknown. The reference stop is used for sizing, not as an auto-exit; simulated drawdown can exceed the planned stop-risk. 5% position and 20% portfolio notional caps still apply. Observation trials are excluded from strategy win-rate stats; manual close remains available.
