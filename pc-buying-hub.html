Common questions

What should I decide first?

Start with your use case, total budget and target resolution. For a gaming build, the CPU/GPU balance matters more than chasing every premium specification.

Why do prices on PC parts change so much?

Retail prices, promotions, inventory and seller availability can change. Treat displayed deal prices as a snapshot and confirm the retailer page before buying.

Does PCDealHub publish every cheap listing?

No. The public feed is intentionally limited to listings that pass the site's verification requirements. A low price by itself is not enough.
