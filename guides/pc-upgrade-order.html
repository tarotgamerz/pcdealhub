What Should You Upgrade First?

A simple method for choosing the next PC component without wasting money.

Identify the current limit

Use real workload data: frame rate, GPU utilization, CPU load, RAM pressure, storage capacity and temperatures.

Fix the biggest constraint

Upgrading a part that is already keeping up will rarely produce a meaningful improvement.

Consider platform cost

A CPU upgrade can sometimes require a new motherboard or RAM. Compare the total platform cost.

Set an outcome

Define what success means: higher FPS, smoother frame times, faster loading, more storage or fewer crashes.

PCDealHub principle: Buy for your actual workload and verify the listing, seller, price and terms before purchasing.
