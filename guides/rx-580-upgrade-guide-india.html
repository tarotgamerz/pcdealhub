RX 580 Upgrade Guide for India

Still using an RX 580-class graphics card? The upgrade decision is less about chasing the newest GPU and more about matching the card to your CPU, PSU, monitor and budget.

1. Decide what is limiting you

At 1080p, an old CPU can become the limit after a GPU upgrade. Check CPU usage, GPU usage, frame-time stability and temperatures before buying a faster card.

2. Check your PSU first

Record the exact new GPU's recommended PSU and power connectors. Do not assume a wattage number is universal across every board-partner model.

3. Check physical fit

Measure the free GPU length and thickness inside your case. Some triple-fan cards are much longer than older dual-fan RX 580 models.

4. New versus used

A used GPU can lower the upfront cost, but you need to inspect temperatures, fan behavior, artifacting, the seller's history and remaining warranty. A new card normally gives a clearer return and warranty path.

5. Compare exact models

Do not compare “RX 7600” to “RX 7600” without checking the board-partner model. Cooler design, dimensions, clock profile, ports and warranty can differ.
