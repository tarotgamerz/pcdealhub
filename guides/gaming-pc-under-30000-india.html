Gaming PC Under ₹30,000 in India

At ₹30,000, the goal is balance. A practical gaming build needs to leave money for every essential part rather than putting nearly the whole budget into one component.

These are planning ranges, not live product prices. Actual availability and component pricing can make one build very different from another.

Where people usually go wrong

  • Spending the budget on the GPU and then cutting the PSU or motherboard too far.
  • Choosing a CPU/GPU combination that leaves a major bottleneck for the games being targeted.
  • Buying only one RAM stick when the platform would benefit from a matched dual-channel kit.
  • Forgetting the cost of a usable SSD, case, cooling and required power connectors.
