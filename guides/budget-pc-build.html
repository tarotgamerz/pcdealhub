How to Allocate a PC Build Budget

A simple framework for balancing gaming performance, reliability and upgrade flexibility.

Set the target first

Choose the games, resolution and frame-rate target before selecting individual parts.

Protect the GPU budget

For a gaming-focused build, graphics performance is often a major part of the overall experience, so avoid overspending on cosmetic extras first.

Do not sacrifice the basics

Use a suitable PSU, adequate cooling and reliable storage instead of squeezing every rupee into one component.

Plan the next upgrade

A balanced build can be easier to improve later when the motherboard, PSU and case leave sensible upgrade paths.
