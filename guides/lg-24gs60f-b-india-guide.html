Core specifications to verify

The retailer listing identifies the 24GS60F-B as a 23.8-inch Full HD 1920×1080 IPS monitor with a 180Hz maximum refresh rate, 1ms GtG response time, HDR10 support, sRGB 99% (typ.), AMD FreeSync and NVIDIA G-SYNC Compatible support. It has HDMI 2.0 and DisplayPort 1.4 inputs, plus a headphone output.

Why 180Hz matters

A high refresh rate can make motion look smoother when your PC can actually deliver high frame rates. At 1080p, the monitor is a natural fit for gaming systems targeting competitive or high-frame-rate play. You do not need to hit 180 FPS all the time to use the display, but the benefit becomes more noticeable as frame rate rises.

Check your GPU output

Before ordering, make sure your graphics card has the required output and that you are using a suitable cable. The retailer specification lists DisplayPort 1.4 and HDMI 2.0. For a 180Hz setup, check the exact connection mode and settings available on your GPU.
