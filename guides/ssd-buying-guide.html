How to Choose an SSD for a Gaming PC

Understand capacity, interface and workload before paying for premium specifications.

SATA vs NVMe

SATA SSDs are still useful for upgrades, while NVMe drives connect through PCIe and can offer much higher sequential bandwidth.

Capacity first

Games are large. A smaller premium drive can be frustrating if you constantly run out of space. Plan your game library before choosing the size.

Do not confuse peak speed with game performance

Synthetic sequential numbers do not always translate into equally large gaming gains. Random access and game workload behavior matter.

Keep free space

Leaving reasonable free capacity helps a drive maintain performance and makes updates easier.

PCDealHub principle: Buy for your actual workload and verify the listing, seller, price and terms before purchasing.
