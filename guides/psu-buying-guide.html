PSU Guide for a Gaming PC

A reliable power supply protects the rest of your build and should be chosen from the whole system's needs.

Estimate the whole system

GPU and CPU are the largest power consumers, but drives, fans, USB devices and transient loads matter too.

Prefer quality over exaggerated wattage

A reputable, appropriately rated unit is generally a better target than an unknown high-wattage model.

Check the connectors

Make sure the PSU provides the connectors your GPU and motherboard actually require, including any newer GPU power standards.

Leave sensible headroom

You do not need to buy an extreme wattage unit just because a calculator gives a peak number. Aim for practical headroom without paying for unused capacity.

PCDealHub principle: Buy for your actual workload and verify the listing, seller, price and terms before purchasing.
