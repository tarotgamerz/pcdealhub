RX 7600 8GB Buying Guide for India

A practical guide for comparing RX 7600 graphics cards without confusing the GPU model with the exact board-partner card.

What you are actually buying

The RX 7600 is the GPU family. The final product can differ by board partner, cooler, factory clock, dimensions and power connector. Always compare the exact model number before treating two prices as equivalent.

What to check before checkout

  • Exact model: record the full board-partner model code.
  • VRAM: this class uses 8GB GDDR6; make sure that matches your target games and settings.
  • Power: the exact card can specify its own recommended PSU and connector. Check the manufacturer's listing.
  • Case fit: triple-fan cards can be substantially longer than compact cards.
  • Warranty: confirm the India warranty route and keep the GST invoice.
