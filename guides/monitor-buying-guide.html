How to Choose a Gaming Monitor

Refresh rate, resolution, panel behavior and connectivity all affect the experience.

Match the monitor to your GPU

A high-refresh monitor is useful when your PC can actually produce enough frames to benefit from it.

Resolution changes GPU demand

Moving from 1080p to 1440p increases the graphics workload. Plan the monitor and graphics card together.

Check response and motion behavior

Look beyond the headline refresh rate. Response behavior, ghosting and overshoot can affect fast games.

Check ports and ergonomics

Display inputs, adjustable stands, VESA support and USB features can matter in daily use.

PCDealHub principle: Buy for your actual workload and verify the listing, seller, price and terms before purchasing.
