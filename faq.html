PCDealHub FAQ

Straight answers about deals, prices and affiliate links.

Are the deals guaranteed to be live?

No. A listing is checked before publication, but retailer price, stock, shipping, warranty and promotion terms can change. Always confirm the retailer checkout page before buying.

How do you choose the reference price?

We aim to compare against a reasonable recent market price rather than blindly using an inflated MRP. The exact reference method can vary by product and availability.

Does PCDealHub sell the products?

No. Retailers handle checkout, shipping, returns and warranty. PCDealHub is a discovery and comparison service.

Do affiliate links cost the customer more?

Normally no. A retailer or affiliate network may pay PCDealHub a commission on a qualifying action while the customer sees the retailer's normal checkout price.

Why are there sometimes no deals?

We would rather show an empty verified feed than publish questionable or stale listings. The site is designed around that rule.

Can I submit a deal?

Yes. Use the deal-submission form from the homepage or email the listing URL, product name, seller and price to tarotgamerz@gmail.com.

What does a score mean?

It is an editorial value indicator based on the deal's price/value and verification information. It is not a guarantee of future performance or a substitute for your own product requirements.
