PCDealHub verification standard

How we verify a deal

A lower number is not automatically a good deal. PCDealHub is designed to check the listing itself before putting it into the public deal feed.

The checks

1. Actual listing

We need a real product page or other reliable source, not a copied headline or screenshot with no source.

2. Seller

The seller or store should be identifiable so shoppers know where the transaction is happening.

3. Current price

The published price should come from the source checked at the time of verification.

4. Availability

Stock or availability is checked when the listing is reviewed. Stock can change afterwards.

5. Product identity

Model, capacity, variant and other key specifications are checked so a cheap different variant is not mistaken for the target product.

6. Condition & terms

New, used, refurbished, warranty, returns and other important conditions should be clear before publication.

How the reference price is handled

A deal score can use a reference price, but a reference price is not treated as proof of a discount by itself. When possible, the reference should come from a credible comparable listing or documented price history. We avoid inventing an “old price” just to make a percentage discount look bigger.

Affiliate transparency: PCDealHub may earn a commission from qualifying purchases through affiliate links. That does not replace the verification checks above, and it does not mean every listing on a retailer is recommended.

What “verified” does not mean

  • It does not guarantee that the price will stay the same after publication.
  • It does not guarantee stock, delivery time, warranty service or retailer performance after the check.
  • It does not mean the product is right for every buyer or every PC build.
Prices move fast. Always open the retailer page and confirm the final price, shipping, seller, stock and terms before paying.
