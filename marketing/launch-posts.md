## Reddit / PC building communities
**Title:** I built a free PC deals + buying-guide site for India

I got tired of comparing PC parts across different Indian stores, so I built PCDealHub.

It currently has:
- Verified PC deal listings with price/seller/stock checks
- PC buying guides for GPUs, CPUs, SSDs, RAM, PSUs and monitors
- Budget-specific pages like SSD deals under ₹20,000 and gaming monitors under ₹15,000
- A public deal-submission form so useful listings can be checked before publication

The goal is simple: fewer fake “50% OFF” posts and more useful, verifiable buying information.

Website: https://tarotgamerz.github.io/pcdealhub/
Deals: https://tarotgamerz.github.io/pcdealhub/deals.html

Feedback on the site or the deal format is welcome.
