# Research-first skill

Use this playbook whenever the task depends on facts that may be new, niche, uncertain, or likely to have changed.

1. Identify what is unknown.
2. Search authoritative or primary sources first.
3. Record exact dates for time-sensitive claims.
4. Cross-check important claims.
5. Use the evidence to decide the next action.
6. Save durable findings with source and date when they will help future tasks.

Do not browse merely to decorate an answer.
Do not turn search snippets into certainty when the underlying source is unclear.
