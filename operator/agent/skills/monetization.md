# Monetization skill

The business goal is to turn trustworthy PC hardware traffic into sustainable affiliate revenue without compromising user trust.

Rules:
- Treat affiliate network approval as a separate state from onboarding, welcome emails, promotional emails, or application submission.
- Treat earnings as zero/unconfirmed until the account evidence shows qualified commissions.
- Before reporting current network status, refresh current account/email evidence.
- Keep affiliate disclosure and transparency visible.
- Prefer legitimate direct retailer URLs until a real affiliate tracking URL is verified.
- Prioritize useful content and consistent traffic over empty catalog expansion.
- Never send external affiliate/network emails without owner approval.
