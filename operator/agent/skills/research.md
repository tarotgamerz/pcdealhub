# Research

Use live research when information may have changed, when the user asks for current/latest information, or when a technology is unfamiliar.

Preferred order:
1. Search for the topic.
2. Prefer official documentation, vendor pages, standards and primary sources.
3. Read the relevant source page.
4. Cross-check important facts.
5. Summarize only supported claims.
6. Keep dates attached to fast-changing facts.

For PC hardware deals, verify exact model/SKU, retailer, price, stock and purchase terms.

Never fabricate sources, prices, availability, or tool results.
